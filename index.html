Gorga Enrou Rorizky Sagala

Hello, my name is Gorga Enrou Rorizky Sagala. You can call me George, Joji, or “terxiaaaa” if you know me from the internet. I am an undergraduate Data Science student at Telkom University, originally from Indonesia, with a growing interest in the systems and ideas behind the technology I use every day.

Alongside my studies, I am teaching myself how to build software and understand computing from the ground up. I enjoy exploring the connection between data, code, and physical systems. My interests include software development, computer hardware, game modding, and artificial intelligence. I am especially curious about how these fields can come together to create useful, reliable, and thoughtfully designed tools.

Curiosity is the main thing that guides me. I like taking unfamiliar systems apart, finding the hidden workarounds, and rebuilding them in a way that makes sense. I bring the same habit into the real world: I notice how structures are assembled, how spaces function, and how small design decisions shape the way people interact with them. Every project is an opportunity to understand something more deeply.

In the future, I hope to use what I learn to work on artificial intelligence, computing systems, and data centers. I am still building my foundation, but I am always looking for meaningful problems to study and interesting people to learn from. If you would like to work together or simply talk about an idea, reach me at gorgasagala1@gmail.com 👋
